<div> <div> <H2>ESP32-P4-WIFI6 Development Board with Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5 Support</H2> <small>Jul 30, 2025 — by Giorgio Mendoza</small>    <small>5,094 views</small>   <p><img src="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Top.jpg?resize=125%2C98&ssl=1" alt="" width="125" height="98" srcset="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Top.jpg?resize=125%2C98&ssl=1 125w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Top.jpg?resize=450%2C354&ssl=1 450w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Top.jpg?w=647&ssl=1 647w"></p> <p>Waveshare has introduced the ESP32-P4-WIFI6, a multimedia development board built around the ESP32-P4 with an integrated ESP32-C6 module. The design supports Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5/BLE, offering a wide set of human–machine interaction interfaces and hardware expansion capabilities.</p>  <p>The ESP32-P4-WIFI6 combines the processing power of the ESP32-P4 dual-core RISC-V MCU running at 400 MHz with the wireless connectivity of the ESP32-C6, which connects over SDIO to provide Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5. It supports up to 32MB of PSRAM and includes 32MB of onboard NOR flash.</p><div><p>— ADVERTISEMENT —</p>  <br> </div> <p><center><a href="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?ssl=1"><img src="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?resize=301%2C306&ssl=1" alt="" width="301" height="306" srcset="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?resize=443%2C450&ssl=1 443w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?resize=1009%2C1024&ssl=1 1009w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?resize=123%2C125&ssl=1 123w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/esp32-p4-block-diagram.jpg?w=1306&ssl=1 1306w"></a> <br> <strong>ESP32-P4 Block Diagram</strong></center> <center>(click image to enlarge)</center> <br> The platform integrates a variety of multimedia and communication interfaces, such as MIPI-CSI with an Image Signal Processor, MIPI-DSI display output, I2S audio, SPI, I2C, UART, TWAI, ADC, and PWM.</p> <p>In addition, it features a USB 2.0 OTG high-speed port, a microSD card slot using SDIO 3.0, an onboard microphone, and a dedicated header for external speakers. A 40‑pin GPIO expansion header provides additional connectivity and is compatible with some Raspberry Pi Pico HATs.</p> <p><center><a href="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?ssl=1"><img src="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?resize=303%2C303&ssl=1" alt="" width="303" height="303" srcset="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?resize=450%2C450&ssl=1 450w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?resize=125%2C125&ssl=1 125w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?resize=150%2C150&ssl=1 150w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Pinout.jpg?w=657&ssl=1 657w"></a> <br> <strong>ESP32-P4-WIFI6 Pinout</strong></center> <center>(click image to enlarge)</center> <br> The ESP32-P4 integrates a Digital Signature Peripheral and a Key Management Unit to ensure secure boot, flash encryption, and cryptographic operations. Hardware access protection mechanisms further enable access permission management and privilege separation.</p> <p><center><a href="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Peripherals.jpg?ssl=1"><img src="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Peripherals.jpg?resize=332%2C336&ssl=1" alt="" width="332" height="336" srcset="https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Peripherals.jpg?resize=445%2C450&ssl=1 445w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Peripherals.jpg?resize=123%2C125&ssl=1 123w, https://i0.wp.com/linuxgizmos.com/files/ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Peripherals.jpg?w=652&ssl=1 652w"></a> <br> <strong>ESP32-P4-WIFI6 Peripherals</strong></center> <center>(click image to enlarge)</center> <br> According to the product page, the ESP32-P4-WIFI6 also supports AI speech interaction. Using its built-in microphone, the board can send audio input to large model platforms such as DeepSeek and Doubao for processing, returning results that can be displayed on a connected DSI screen and played back through a speaker.</p> <p><strong>Further Information</strong></p> <p>The standard <a href="https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-p4-wifi6.htm?sku=31647">ESP32-P4-WIFI6</a> is offered at $13.99, while the ESP32-P4-WIFI6-M, which includes pre-soldered headers, is available for an additional dollar. For developers needing extra components, the ESP32-P4-WIFI6-KIT-A is priced at $25.99 and comes with accessories such as a speaker, an RPi Camera (B), FFC and camera cables, and a USB connection cable.</p> <p>The ESP32-P4-WIFI6-KIT-B, which expands further with a 10.1‑inch DSI touchscreen display and additional accessories including a DSI cable, adapter cables, and standoffs, is priced at $69.99.</p>    </div>      <center><a href="https://linuxgizmos.com/contact"><font>(advertise here)</font></a></center> <br> <br>   <b><font>PLEASE COMMENT BELOW</font></b>     </div>

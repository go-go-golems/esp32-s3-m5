It supports rich human‑machine interaction interfaces, including MIPI‑CSI (with an integrated Image Signal Processor, ISP) and MIPI‑DSI interfaces, as well as common peripherals such as SPI, I2S, I2C, LED PWM, MCPWM, RMT, ADC, UART, and TWAI. It also features USB OTG 2.0 HS, Ethernet, and SDIO Host 3.0 for high‑speed connectivity. The ESP32-P4 chip integrates a digital signature peripheral and a dedicated key management unit to ensure data and operation security. The ESP32-P4-WIFI6-DEV-KIT is designed for high-performance and high-security applications, and fully meets the higher requirements of embedded applications for human-computer interface support, edge

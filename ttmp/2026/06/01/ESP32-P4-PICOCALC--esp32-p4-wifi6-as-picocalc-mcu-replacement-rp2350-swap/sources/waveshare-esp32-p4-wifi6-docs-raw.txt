The ESP32-P4 uses a 360MHz dual-core RISC-V processor and supports up to 32MB PSRAM, featuring USB 2.0, MIPI-CSI/DSI, H.264 encoding, and other peripherals, meeting the needs for low-cost, high-performance, and low-power multimedia development. In addition, the ESP32-P4 integrates a digital signature peripheral and a dedicated key management unit to ensure data and operation security. The ESP32-P4-WIFI6 is specially designed for high-performance and high-security applications, meeting the needs of embedded systems in human-machine interaction, edge computing, and IO
